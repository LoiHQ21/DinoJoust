# Reusable prompts for style-consistent concept generation

These prompts generate **2D guides for making 3D assets**, not finished FBX/GLB meshes. Always request original designs that do not duplicate characters in existing games.

## Shared style clause (paste into each prompt)

> Original 3D game concept for Jurassic Joust: Crownfall. Bright chibi-anime medieval fantasy with simplified faceted low-to-mid-poly forms, angular/beveled silhouettes, clean hand-painted color blocking, selective gradient shading, limited material detail, readable at mobile landscape gameplay distance, expressive personalities, anatomy-appropriate dinosaur shapes, reusable 3D production mindset. No voxels, no realistic skin microtexture, no copyrighted characters, no existing-game logos, no text overlays unless requested.

## Rider turnaround

> [Style clause] Create an original knight rider with a large expressive chibi head and subtle heroic anime proportions, simple plate armor, a bold crest, gloved hands and a jousting saddle pose. Show front, true left side, true rear, and 3/4 views, consistent armor pieces and colors across views, neutral studio backdrop, no ornate filigree. Add separate detachable helmet/shoulder armor/lance details. Purpose: Blender 3D model reference.

## Dinosaur turnaround

> [Style clause] Create an anatomically recognizable rideable [species], maintaining [signature anatomical traits], fitted with a minimal tournament saddle and armor that does not hide body silhouette. Show front/side/back/3/4 with fixed anatomy and consistent tail/horn dimensions, full body inside each view, separate accessory views and clean neutral background. Designed as a reusable rigged 3D model, not plush toy or block dinosaur.

## Joust combat screenshot

> [Style clause] In-game **true 3D** landscape scene, camera behind and slightly above a red-team rider on a raptor, looking forward down paired opposing joust lanes toward a blue knight on a ceratopsian. Show momentum through fences, dust and parallax, readable enemy torso and lance contact, HUD safe in corners, mid-screen clear. Emphasize position and camera utility over promotional glamour.

## Dinosaur plus rider impact

> [Style clause] A single physically coherent collision in a jousting corridor: red raptor snaps at a blue ceratopsian harness while red knight's lance strikes the blue rider, simultaneous visible causes, exaggerated but readable recoil, helmet and wooden lance fragments fly, non-gory cartoon physics, side replay angle AFTER gameplay contact. Avoid impossible anatomy or dismemberment.

## Arena kit / props

> [Style clause] Modular medieval tournament set for PC/mobile landscape: lane rails, flat stone/mud ground, wall segments, grandstands, banners, towers, tents, breakable hay cart; same low-mid poly faceted materials, clean color groups and predictable scale. Present simple reusable object sheet and an assembled scene.

## Draft / loadout UI

> [Style clause] Landscape menu using large readable fantasy cards with three equipment options (lance, saddle, perk), background 3D model turntable of rider and dinosaur, simple restrained gold/blue/red palette, clear mobile touch targets and no tiny text.

## Validation of generations

Reject inaccurate dinosaur species anatomy, inconsistent turnarounds, irreconcilable accessories, UI covering contact, designs overburdened with texture, excessively round Kingshot-like toys, and anything that would require expensive per-species one-off rigs. Note that AI images can produce nonsense text: UI copy is specified in actual game data, not generated reference art.
